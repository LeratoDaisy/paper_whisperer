# Paper Whisperer: 2-minute pitch

About 280 words. Speak slowly and pause on the demo.

---

**Hook (10 sec)**
Imagine you get a letter that says your water and electricity will be cut in 14 days. It is written in legal English, and English is not the language you speak at home.

**Problem (20 sec)**
Official letters are hard to read. Bills, fines, grant notices and bank messages use formal language that even fluent speakers find confusing. A misunderstood letter means a missed deadline, an extra fee, a lost grant, or a scam that works because the reader is scared and confused.

**Solution (25 sec)**
Meet Paper Whisperer. Take a photo of any letter. In seconds you get what it says in plain words, a short list of what to do and by when, and the key details like the amount and the deadline. It works in all 11 spoken official languages. It can read the answer aloud, it checks every document for scam signs, and you can ask follow-up questions.

**Demo (25 sec)**
*[Tap "Municipal final notice" or scan a real letter.]*
Here is a final notice. Paper Whisperer says: you owe R7 766.75, act by 30 October. Step one, find your ID and payslip. Step two, visit a Customer Care Centre and ask for a payment plan. And here is a bank SMS: it flags it as a likely scam and tells me how to check safely.

**Who it's for (10 sec)**
Parents, grandparents, first-time job seekers, small business owners. Anyone who finds official language hard.

**Why it's better (15 sec)**
Translation apps swap words but don't tell you what to do or by when. Paper Whisperer understands your specific letter, finds the deadline and the amount, and warns you about scams.

**Value (15 sec)**
Free for individuals. Municipalities, banks and NGOs can use it in their customer care to reduce missed payments and queries.

**Close (5 sec)**
Paper Whisperer. Any letter, in plain words. Thank you.
